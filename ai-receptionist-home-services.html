The problem

Why home services businesses lose leads

Calls missed on site

You can't answer the phone halfway through a boiler repair. Every missed call is a job that might go to a competitor.

Emergencies at all hours

Burst pipes and broken heating don't wait for Monday morning. Customers need someone to answer when it happens.

Messy job details

Notes on scraps of paper, details in text messages and nothing in one place make quoting and scheduling harder.

FAQ

Questions from home services businesses

More questions? Ask me directly.

Can it handle emergency calls?

Yes. You define what counts as an emergency, and the agent can transfer those calls to your on-call number or send an instant alert to your team.

Can it check which areas we cover?

Yes. The agent can check postcodes or towns against your service area and politely decline or refer jobs outside it.

Does it work with job management software?

It can connect to CRMs and job tools with an API or webhooks, as well as GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Google Calendar. We check your specific software during discovery.

Can it work for a one-person business?

Yes. That's often where it helps most, because there's nobody else to answer the phone while you work.
