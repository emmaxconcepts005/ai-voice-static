Process

A clear process from first call to launch

Every build starts with the business problem, not the technology. We map the workflow, choose the right tools, connect your systems, test the important paths and document how everything works.

The five steps

What to expect at each stage

  1. Discovery

    We talk through your business: your calls, your leads, the tools you use and where time is being lost.

    What happens

    A conversation (call or chat) where I ask questions about your workflow and goals.

    What I need from you

    A short description of your business, your current tools and the problem you want solved.

    What you get

    A clear picture of what's worth automating, and what isn't.

  2. Solution design

    I map out the workflow and choose the right tools. You see exactly what will be built before any building starts.

    What happens

    I design the call flows, scripts, automations and integrations, and write them up.

    What I need from you

    Feedback on the plan, and approval when you're happy with it.

    What you get

    A written plan and proposal showing what will be built, how it works and what it connects to.

  3. Build and integrate

    The voice agent and automations are built and connected to your CRM, calendar and messaging tools.

    What happens

    I build the agent, write the prompts, set up workflows and connect everything together.

    What I need from you

    Access to the tools being connected, plus your business information (services, FAQs, rules).

    What you get

    A working system in a test environment, with progress updates along the way.

  4. Test

    We run the key scenarios together, including calls that go wrong, and fix what we find.

    What happens

    Scenario testing: normal calls, unusual requests, interruptions, wrong information and handoffs.

    What I need from you

    Time to try the system and tell me what feels off.

    What you get

    A system that has been checked against real situations, with fixes applied.

  5. Deployment and handover

    It goes live with your sign-off, with documentation of how it works.

    What happens

    I switch the system on, monitor the first live calls and make adjustments.

    What I need from you

    Final sign-off.

    What you get

    A live system, plus plain-language documentation covering how it works.

Testing

What we test before going live

Voice agents meet unpredictable people. Testing covers the happy path and the messy reality.

Normal calls

Standard bookings, questions and enquiries from start to finish.

Unusual requests

Questions outside the script, and how the agent responds.

Edge cases

Interruptions, unclear answers, wrong details and changed minds.

Handoffs

Transfers, messages and escalation to your team.

Integrations

Data reaching the CRM, calendar and messaging correctly.

Failure paths

What happens if a connected tool is down or slow.

After launch

Launch is the start, not the end

Real calls teach you things testing can't. After going live, systems are reviewed and refined so they keep getting better.

After go-live

  • Early live calls reviewed for issues
  • Prompts and scripts refined based on real conversations
  • Workflows adjusted as your process changes
  • New integrations and features added when you need them
  • Documentation updated alongside changes

FAQ

Questions about the process

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused single-purpose agent is much quicker than a multi-system build with several integrations. You'll get a timeline in the proposal after discovery.

Do I have to approve the plan before you start?

Yes. Nothing is built until you've seen and approved the solution design.

What do you need access to?

Only the tools being connected, such as your CRM, calendar or messaging accounts. Access requirements are listed in the plan, and you stay in control of your accounts.

Will I be able to make changes myself?

Simple changes, yes. The documentation explains how the system works and what can be adjusted safely. Bigger changes can be handled as follow-up work.

What if something goes wrong after launch?

Get in touch and we'll look at what happened. The early live period is specifically for spotting and fixing issues.
