How it works

Every build starts with the business problem. We map the workflow, choose the right tools, connect the systems, test the important paths and document how it works.

  1. Discovery

    We talk through your calls, leads, tools and where time is lost.

  2. Solution design

    I map the workflow and choose the tools, and you approve the plan before building.

  3. Build and integrate

    The agent or automation is built and connected to your CRM, calendar and messaging.

  4. Test

    We run the key scenarios together, including calls that go wrong, and fix what we find.

  5. Deployment and handover

    It goes live with your sign-off, with documentation of how it works.
