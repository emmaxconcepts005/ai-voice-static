The problem

Where holiday sales quietly slip away

Small stores rarely lose holiday sales because of the products. They lose them in the gaps between a shopper's question and an answer.

Questions nobody answers in time

"Will it arrive before Christmas?" "Is this in stock?" "Can I return it after the holidays?" A shopper who waits hours for a reply often buys somewhere else.

Carts left behind

Plenty of shoppers add items and leave to compare prices or finish later. Without a timely, friendly reminder, most of them never come back.

A small team at the busiest time

In December you're packing orders, not watching your inbox. Messages pile up exactly when they matter most.

FAQ

Common questions

Which store platforms does it work with?

Shopify and WooCommerce are the main ones. Squarespace, Wix, BigCommerce and other platforms are possible too; I'll confirm what's possible for your store before we start.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

For WhatsApp replies, yes. I'll help you set it up, or we can start with SMS only.

What does it cost to run?

Messaging platforms charge small per-message fees on your own accounts. I'll explain the running cost for your volume before anything is set up.

Will it reply to customers in a way I don't agree with?

It only answers from the FAQ and policies you approve. Anything else is handed to you with the conversation so far.

What happens after the holidays?

Keep it running all year with your regular FAQ, or switch it off. Everything runs on accounts in your name.

Want to see it for your store?

Send me your store link and I'll put together a quick example of the answers and cart reminder for your products.
