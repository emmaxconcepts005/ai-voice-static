The problem

Why pest control businesses lose leads

Calls missed in the field

Technicians are under houses and in attics, not by the phone. Calls during the working day go to voicemail, and most callers won't leave one.

Evening and weekend enquiries

Pest problems don't keep office hours. People search and call in the evening, when nobody is there to book them in.

Same questions every day

"Do you treat bed bugs?", "Is it safe for pets?", "How much is an inspection?" Answering them over and over eats your day.

FAQ

Questions from pest control businesses

More questions? Ask me directly.

Can it tell urgent jobs apart from routine ones?

Yes. You decide what counts as urgent, such as wasp nests near entrances or a large infestation, and the agent can flag those, transfer the call or create a priority callback task.

Will it quote prices?

Only if you want it to. It can give price ranges you approve, or explain that pricing is confirmed after inspection.

Can it book into my existing scheduling system?

Usually, yes. It works with calendars and CRMs such as GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Google Calendar and other tools with an API or webhooks. We confirm your setup during discovery.

What about commercial clients?

The agent can ask whether a caller is residential or commercial and route commercial enquiries to you or your sales process.
