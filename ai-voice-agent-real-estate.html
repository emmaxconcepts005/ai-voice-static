The problem

Why real estate businesses lose leads

Slow lead response

Portal leads go cold quickly. If it takes hours to call back, the buyer has already booked viewings elsewhere.

Unqualified enquiries

Agents spend hours on calls with people who aren't ready, can't afford it or are just browsing.

Viewing admin

Back-and-forth over viewing times, confirmations and no-shows eats into time for selling.

FAQ

Questions from real estate businesses

More questions? Ask me directly.

Can it talk about specific listings?

Yes, using the property details you provide or connect. It only answers from approved information and passes detailed questions to an agent.

Can it call portal leads automatically?

Yes. An outbound agent can be triggered when a new lead arrives, so they get a call within moments rather than hours.

Which CRMs does it work with?

GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho and other systems with an API or webhooks. We check your setup during discovery.
