The problem

Where service businesses quietly lose time and money

Most businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a response problem. Calls go unanswered, follow-up slips, and the same information gets typed into three different tools.

Missed and after-hours calls

When nobody picks up, callers rarely leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list. Every missed call can be a missed job.

Slow follow-up

Leads go cold fast. If it takes hours or days to get back to an enquiry, the customer has often already booked with someone else.

Copy-and-paste admin

Details are retyped from calls to spreadsheets to CRMs to calendars. It's slow, it's boring and it's where mistakes creep in.

The fix

A system that answers, captures, books and follows up

Instead of adding more people to answer phones and chase leads, you add a reliable system that handles the repetitive parts of every conversation and passes the rest to your team with full context.

Without automation

  • Calls missed during busy hours and evenings
  • Leads waiting hours for a reply
  • Notes scattered across apps
  • Follow-up depends on memory
  • Staff stuck on repetitive questions

With EMMAX

  • Calls answered and routed consistently
  • Instant, structured lead capture
  • Data flows straight into your CRM
  • Reminders and follow-ups trigger automatically
  • Your team handles the conversations that matter

Inside a call

What happens when a customer calls your AI agent

A typical inbound booking flow. Every step can be adjusted to match your rules, your tools and your tone of voice.

  1. Answers

    The agent picks up straight away with your business greeting, even when your team is busy.

  2. Understands

    It works out what the caller needs and answers common questions from your approved information.

  3. Captures

    Name, number, address, service needed and any details you care about are collected in a structured way.

  4. Books or routes

    It checks your calendar and books a slot, or transfers urgent calls to the right person.

  5. Updates & follows up

    Your CRM is updated, your team is notified and confirmation messages go out automatically.

FAQ

Quick answers

Have a question that isn't here? Send me a message.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

Modern voice agents sound natural, but I recommend being transparent with callers. How the agent introduces itself is something we agree on together during design.

What happens if the AI can't handle a call?

You decide. The agent can transfer the call to a person, take a message, or create a task for your team to call back, depending on how it's set up.

Can it work with my existing CRM and calendar?

Usually, yes. I work with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and webhook-based systems. We confirm what your setup supports during discovery.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. I handle the build and the connections, and I explain everything in plain language. You'll get documentation showing how your system works.

How do we get started?

Fill in the contact form or message me on WhatsApp with a short description of your business and what you want to automate. We'll set up a discovery chat from there.
