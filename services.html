Common questions

Can the agent connect to my CRM?

Usually yes. I work with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and webhook-based systems. We confirm what your setup supports during discovery.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, where it is connected to your calendar. It can check availability, book, reschedule or cancel, depending on how it is configured.

Do you build inbound and outbound calling?

Both. I build flows for incoming calls and outbound calling workflows.

Which voice platforms do you use?

Mainly Retell AI and Vapi, with OpenAI and telephony integrations such as Twilio.
