01 · Voice

AI voice agents & virtual receptionists

FAQ

Common questions about services

Still unsure what you need? Describe your business and I'll suggest a starting point.

Can the agent connect to my CRM?

Usually yes. I work with GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and webhook-based systems. We confirm what your setup supports during discovery.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, when it's connected to your calendar. It can check availability, book, reschedule or cancel, depending on how it's configured.

Do you build inbound and outbound calling?

Both. I build flows for incoming calls as well as outbound calling workflows for follow-up, confirmations and lead qualification.

Which voice platforms do you use?

Mainly Retell AI and Vapi, with OpenAI models and telephony integrations such as Twilio. The right platform depends on your requirements.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

In many cases calls can be forwarded or routed from your existing number to the agent. We check what's possible with your phone provider during discovery.

Can the agent transfer calls to a real person?

Yes. Urgent calls, complex questions or specific requests can be transferred to you or a team member, based on rules you set.

Which automation platform should I use: n8n, Make.com or Zapier?

It depends on your tools, the complexity of your workflows and your budget. I'll recommend the best fit during solution design, and explain why.

Do you only work with certain industries?

No. Service businesses that rely on calls and bookings get the most from this work, but the same approach suits sales teams, agencies and many other businesses.

Not sure what fits?

Describe your business and what's slowing you down. I'll recommend where to start.
