The problem

Why clinics businesses lose leads

Front desk overload

No-shows and late cancellations

Empty appointment slots cost money, and manual reminder calls take hours every week.

Repetitive questions

Opening hours, prices, parking and "do you take my insurance?" come up constantly.

FAQ

Questions from clinics businesses

More questions? Ask me directly.

Will it give medical advice?

No. The agent is set up to handle admin only: bookings, reschedules and general questions. Clinical or sensitive questions are passed to your staff.

How is patient information handled?

The agent only collects what's needed for the booking. Data handling and the tools used are agreed with you during design, so they fit your privacy obligations.

Can it reduce no-shows?

Automated confirmations and reminders by call, SMS or WhatsApp, with easy rescheduling, are a common way to reduce missed appointments.

Does it work with my booking software?

It can connect to calendars and booking tools with an API or webhooks. We check your specific software during discovery.
